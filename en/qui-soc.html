About me

Who is behind L'Abelluco

Right now, L'Abelluco is one person: me, Mario Escuder. Alongside this project, I'm a secondary school teacher.

I'm a software engineer and a forest and natural environment management technician. I have also trained as an environmental educator and in permaculture, organic farming and GIS tools.

I live in Olba, in the Mijares valley, in the Gúdar-Javalambre district (Teruel). From here I bring together two things that often go separately: digital tools and caring for the land.

My vision

I believe the land needs people who know it and care for it, and that it has to be seen as a whole: forests, water, crops, landscape and the people who live there.

That's why I combine natural environment management, which looks after it with ecological criteria, and environmental education, which helps people understand it, value it and get involved.

Technology is one more tool, not the centre: small, useful and easy to understand, without making anyone depend on a big platform. Open data, maps and apps can help make better decisions if they are made alongside the people who live there.

Transparency

Ten points on how I use AI

I use artificial intelligence in part of my work. These are the commitments I make when I do.

  1. I say so openly

    I use AI and I don't hide it, either on this site or in my projects.

  2. It poses a dilemma for me

    Because of its energy use, copyright, the concentration of power and its effect on many people's jobs. I don't have the answer, which is why I use it sparingly.

  3. Balanced, mindful use

    I use it when it genuinely helps, not out of habit or to go faster at any cost.

  4. It isn't right for everything

    I use it for specific tasks: writing and reviewing code, documentation, translations or drafts of texts and materials. I don't use it to replace knowledge of the land, fieldwork or dealing with people.

  5. The decisions are mine

    What gets made, for whom, how it should work and what it should look like: I make the creative and strategic decisions.

  6. I review and check everything it does

    I review everything it does: I read the code and test it with real cases, check the calculations and the data, and correct the texts before I sign them off.

  7. I am accountable

    The result is my responsibility. If something goes wrong, there is a person behind it who fixes it.

  8. Your data, with permission

    I don't give a client's personal or confidential data to any AI service without their consent.

  9. Free software, whenever possible

    When the project allows it, the code is published under a free licence and anyone can review what it does and how.

  10. A living charter

    AI changes fast. I will revise these commitments whenever needed, and I'll do it here, in the open.
