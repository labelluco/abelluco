Terra Rutes: trails & GPX

Terra Rutes is the app for your walking, cycling and riding routes: open them, record them, follow them on the ground and edit them your way.

Open the app All the apps

Works in your phone or computer browser, with nothing to install. Coming soon to Google Play.

What you can do

  • Record and follow

    • Record your track with GPS, with pause and waypoints (on Android, even with the screen off)
    • Follow a route: where you are, how far you have come, how much is left, the climb and descent to go and the estimated time
    • Off-route alert, with a line to the nearest point
    • Works without coverage: the route is already on your phone

  • Open and edit

    • Open GPX and KML tracks, several at once
    • Join them: the app suggests the order and direction
    • Move, delete or add points, trim the start or the end, split the track and clean GPS jumps, with undo

  • Know your route

    • Elevation profile coloured by slope, synced with the map
    • Distance, elevation gain and loss, altitudes, slopes and estimated time with the MIDE method
    • Elevations from the IGN terrain model (5 m in Spain), far more reliable than GPS altitude
    • The route on 3D terrain, with aerial imagery
    • Which Spanish Cadastre parcels it crosses and which sections run on public forests and drove roads (for guidance)

  • Routes from town councils and organisations

    • Open with a link or a QR code the routes offered by town councils, tourist offices and accommodation

  • Private

    • No accounts, no ads and no analytics
    • Your routes and your location are stored only on your device

Free, with a one-time Pro purchase

Recording, following, 3D view and up to three routes are free. The Pro version (one-time purchase, no subscription) adds unlimited routes, advanced editing, GPX export and the Cadastre check.

Data is for guidance only and comes from public sources. The estimated time does not take into account the type of path, the weather or fitness.

Data sources

  • Cadastre: Spain's Directorate General for the Cadastre
  • Terrain model and PNOA aerial imagery: National Geographic Institute of Spain (IGN)
  • Public forests and drove roads: Spanish Inventory of Natural Heritage and Biodiversity (MITECO)
