Terra Anàlisi: slope analysis

Terra Anàlisi analyses the terrain of any piece of land: a plot, a farm, a mountain or an area you draw on the map.

For farming, forestry, engineering, surveys and appraisals, or simply to get to know your land better.

Open the app All the apps

Works in your phone or computer browser, with nothing to install. Coming soon to Google Play.

What you can do

  • Choose the area your way

    • By cadastral reference, or province, municipality, polygon and parcel number (Spain)
    • By drawing a rectangle or a free shape anywhere in the world
    • By searching for a place by name

  • In a few seconds you get

    • Elevation and slope maps over aerial imagery
    • A terrain summary: minimum, maximum and mean elevation, elevation range, and mean and maximum slope
    • A 3D view of the terrain that you can rotate and zoom

  • With the Pro version

    • Aspect and hillshade maps
    • Area by slope class and by aspect
    • PDF report with maps, legends and tables
    • Unlimited saved areas

  • Open, quality data

    • In Spain, the Cadastre, the IGN terrain model and PNOA aerial photos
    • Everywhere else, open elevation data and Sentinel-2 imagery
    • No accounts, no ads and no analytics
    • Your areas are stored only on your device

Free, with a one-time Pro purchase

Analysing areas, elevation and slope maps, the 3D view and up to three saved areas are free. The Pro version is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Results are for guidance only and come from public data: they are not valid for official procedures and do not replace a topographic survey.

Data sources

  • Cadastre: Spain's Directorate General for the Cadastre
  • Terrain model and PNOA aerial imagery: National Geographic Institute of Spain (IGN)
