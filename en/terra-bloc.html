Terra Bloc: 3D terrain

Terra Bloc turns any piece of land into a 3D model: a plot, a mountain, a valley or your home town.

In a few seconds you get the model in 3D, with aerial imagery on top, and you can rotate it, zoom in and exaggerate the relief.

Open the app All the apps

Works in your phone or computer browser, with nothing to install. Coming soon to Google Play.

What you can do

  • Choose the area your way

    • By cadastral reference, or polygon and parcel number (Spain)
    • By tapping a parcel on the map
    • By drawing a rectangle or a free shape anywhere in the world
    • By searching for a place by name

  • Made for 3D printing

    • Print-ready version: a closed solid with a base, at the size you choose in millimetres
    • STL, 3MF and OBJ export; mosaics of up to 10 × 10 pieces
    • OBJ centred or in real-world coordinates, to fit into CAD and GIS software

  • Open, quality data

    • In Spain, the Cadastre, the IGN terrain model and PNOA aerial photos
    • Everywhere else, open elevation data and Sentinel-2 imagery

  • Private

    • No accounts, no ads and no analytics
    • Your areas are stored only on your device

Free, with a one-time Pro purchase

Making models and viewing them in 3D is free. The Pro version, a one-time purchase with no subscription, lets you export STL, 3MF and OBJ files.

Pro price: €4.99 (VAT included), one-time payment.

Models are for guidance only and come from public data: they are not valid for official procedures and do not replace a topographic survey.

Data sources

  • Cadastre: Spain's Directorate General for the Cadastre
  • Terrain model and PNOA aerial imagery: National Geographic Institute of Spain (IGN)
