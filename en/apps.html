How they work

  • Free, with a one-off Pro purchase

    Every app can be used for free. Advanced features unlock with a single payment, no subscriptions.

  • Your data stays on your device

    No accounts and no servers of our own. Your areas, tracks and parcels are saved only on your phone or computer.

  • Public, open data

    Terrain and imagery come from IGN and the Cadastre in Spain, and from open global sources everywhere else.

  • Nothing to install

    They open in your browser and can be added to your home screen. Coming soon to Google Play.

Free software, whenever possible

We support free software: we use free tools, contribute to them and, whenever the project allows it, publish the code under a free licence.

The apps have a free version for everyone and a one-off Pro purchase. That is what keeps the project sustainable and growing, with fair prices and no subscriptions.
