The apps

  • Terra Bloc: the terrain, as a block you can print

    Pick a Spanish Cadastre parcel or draw an area anywhere in the world. Terra Bloc builds a 3D model with the aerial image and turns it into a solid piece for your 3D printer, sized in millimetres.

    • 5 m terrain from IGN in Spain and ~30 m everywhere else
    • Aerial image, colour by altitude or scale-model look
    • Closed solid with a base, at the scale you choose
    • STL and OBJ export with Terra Bloc Pro
    Learn more Open Terra Bloc

  • Terra Rutes: your routes, on the terrain

    Open GPX or KML tracks and view them on the map and the 3D terrain, with an elevation profile coloured by slope. Join them, edit them and correct their altitudes with the terrain model.

    • Distance, elevation gain and estimated MIDE time
    • Join tracks in the right order and direction
    • Move points, trim and clean up GPS jumps
    • GPX export for your GPS, phone or Wikiloc
    Learn more Open Terra Rutes

  • Terra Parcel·les: your parcels, always at hand

    Save your Cadastre parcels once, with notes, photos and tags, group them into estates and open them in the other Terra apps.

    • By cadastral reference or by tapping it on the map
    • Area, crops and a link to the Cadastre's website
    • Estates and groups, with the total area
    • Boundary as GPX or KML to walk it in the field
    Learn more Open Terra Parcel·les

  • Terra Anàlisi: a plot's terrain, in maps and figures

    Choose a cadastral parcel or draw an area anywhere in the world. Terra Anàlisi makes elevation and slope maps over aerial imagery, a terrain summary and a 3D view.

    • 5 m terrain from IGN in Spain and ~30 m everywhere else
    • Elevations, elevation range, and mean and maximum slope
    • A 3D view of the terrain that you can rotate and zoom
    • Aspect, hillshade, area by class and PDF report with Terra Anàlisi Pro
    Learn more Open Terra Anàlisi

  • Terra Camp: measure your plot and compare it with the Cadastre

    Walk your plot with your phone and take each corner with the GPS. Terra Camp works out its area and compares it with the Cadastre's: you see at once whether the boundaries in the field match the map.

    • Several GPS readings averaged at each corner
    • Area difference in m² and %, shown on the map
    • 3D terrain view with the parcel and your measurement
    • Export and PDF report with Terra Camp Pro
    Learn more Open Terra Camp
