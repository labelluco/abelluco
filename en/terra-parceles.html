Terra Parcel·les: land plots

Terra Parcel·les is where you keep your land: the cadastral plots you own, work or want to keep an eye on (Spain).

Each plot has its own sheet with the public Cadastre data: official and calculated area, perimeter, land use and crops.

Open the app All the apps

Works in your phone or computer browser, with nothing to install. Coming soon to Google Play.

What you can do

  • Add them your way

    • By cadastral reference
    • By province, municipality, polygon and parcel number
    • By tapping the plot on the map, over the aerial photo

  • Organise them your way

    • Tags (olives, irrigated, rented…) to filter the list
    • Notes: boundaries, who works it, when it was pruned
    • Photos of each plot
    • Groups: an estate, an inheritance or a hunting ground, with the total area

  • Take them to the field

    • A PDF report of each plot, with the plot over the aerial photo, a location map, your notes and photos
    • Download the boundary as a GPX or KML track and walk it with your GPS
    • Open the plot in the other Terra apps: Terra Camp, Terra Rutes, Terra Anàlisi and Terra Bloc

  • Your data is yours

    • No accounts, no ads and no analytics
    • Everything is stored only on your device
    • Backup to a file, to move them to another phone

Free, with a one-time Pro purchase

The free version keeps 5 plots and one group, with tags, notes and boundary export. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription: unlimited plots and groups, photos, the PDF report, exporting a whole group and the CSV list.

Pro price: €2.99 (VAT included), one-time payment.

The data comes from the Spanish Cadastre and is for guidance only: it is not valid for official boundary procedures.

Data sources

  • Cadastre: Spain's Directorate General for the Cadastre
  • PNOA aerial imagery and IGN-Base map: National Geographic Institute of Spain (IGN)
