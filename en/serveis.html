Services

Technology and the natural environment in service of the land

Digital tools, environmental education and natural environment management for associations, cooperatives, research centres, public bodies and people who live and work in rural areas.

Technology

  • Apps for Google Play

    Publishing apps on Google Play, with a free version and paid features if needed, and maintenance after launch.

  • Cross-platform apps

    A single codebase that runs in the browser, on Android, on iOS and on the computer, offline in the field too.

  • Custom apps

    Tools made for your work:

    • maps and viewers for land data
    • field forms and inventories
    • catalogues and databases

  • Electronics projects

    Sensors, monitoring stations, automation and educational robotics with Arduino, micro:bit or Compluino, from prototype to installation.

  • New technologies advice

    Help choosing, setting up and learning to use digital tools, with a preference for free software.

Environmental education and the natural environment

  • Environmental education advice

    Design of programmes, activities and materials.

  • Forest and natural environment management

    Inventories, mapping and GIS, terrain analysis and support for land stewardship.

How I work: the values of the social and solidarity economy

  • Free software

    The code I write is free whenever possible: the project is yours and you don't depend on me to carry it on.

  • Fair, transparent prices

    Clear quotes, no surprises, adapted to the size of each organisation.

  • Rooted in rural life

    I live and work in the countryside. I am aware of the needs of the land.

  • Cooperation and sustainability

    I prefer to work with other social economy projects and to build lightweight tools that last and use little energy.

I am a member of Smart Ibérica, a social economy worker cooperative: commissions and apps are invoiced through the cooperative.
